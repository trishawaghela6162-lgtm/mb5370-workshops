``` ### CheckM results and interpretation

CheckM v1.2.2 was used to estimate genome-bin completeness and contamination using lineage-specific marker genes.

The analysis evaluated seven genome files, including six MetaBAT2 bins and the separately extracted contig_43.

The highest-quality genome had an estimated completeness of 99.87% and contamination of 0.71%.

Another bin, metabat.5, had 67.68% completeness and 0.00% detected contamination.

Several other bins had low completeness (7.48–18.26%), indicating that substantial portions of their estimated genomes were missing.

Overall, the metagenomic assembly and binning recovered genomes of varying quality. Low detected contamination does not necessarily indicate a complete genome.
